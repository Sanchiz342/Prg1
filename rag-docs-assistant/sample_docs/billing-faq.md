# Billing FAQ

> Demo document. Zirka Tasks is a fictional product invented for this project.

## Which plans are available?

The Free plan includes up to 3 projects and up to 5 members per workspace.
The Team plan costs $8 per member per month when billed annually, or $10 when billed monthly.
The Business plan costs $15 per member per month and adds SSO, audit logs and priority support.

## Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every new workspace gets a 14-day trial of the Business plan. No credit card is required to start the trial.
When the trial ends, the workspace moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Annual Business plans can also be paid by bank transfer.
Invoices are sent by email to the billing contact and can be downloaded from Settings › Billing.

## Can I get a refund?

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 14 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refunded, but you can cancel
at any time and keep access until the end of the paid period.

## What happens when I add or remove members?

Adding a member in the middle of a billing period is charged pro rata. Removing a member credits the unused time to
your next invoice; credits are not paid out as cash.

## What happens to my data if I cancel?

After cancellation your workspace becomes read-only for 30 days. During that time you can export all data as JSON
or CSV. After 30 days the workspace and its data are permanently deleted.

## Do you offer discounts?

Registered non-profit organisations and schools get a 50% discount on the Team plan. Contact support with proof
of status to apply the discount.
